G-SMaRT Accounting - version 37 (2027-01-07)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 36.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
No database update this time: no new migration, no table or column change, and the
bundled database files are unchanged.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the page script and the
style sheet changed).

What is new
-----------
1. "Add new" in the drop-downs of the transactions
 - The Customer, Vendor, name, Account and Item boxes end their list with an
   "Add new ..." line. With a name typed it reads, for example,
   Add "Northwind Packaging" as a new supplier.
 - Choosing it opens the list's own form in a window over the transaction, with the
   typed name filled in. Save: the window closes, the new record is chosen in the box
   and added to the other boxes of the same kind on the page (and to the new lines).
   Cancel or x: nothing is added. The transaction is never left.
 - Customers: invoices, estimates, sales orders, sales receipts, statement charges,
   credit memos, receive payments, the Customer:Job of purchase lines, the POS.
 - Suppliers: purchase orders, item receipts, bills, supplier debit memos, receive
   items and enter bills, advances to suppliers.
 - Customers and suppliers (name boxes): Pay to the order of on checks, Paid to on
   cash vouchers, Name / SL on journal entries and cash vouchers, Customer /
   Supplier on the Expenses tab.
 - Accounts: the Expenses tab, journal entries, cash vouchers, deposits, the bank
   account of a check, deposit to, transfers, processing costs. A box that holds only
   some kinds of account (Bank and Credit Card) offers only those kinds.
 - Items: the item lines of purchase orders, item receipts, bills, checks, supplier
   debit memos, invoices, estimates, sales orders, sales receipts, credit memos, and
   the outputs of Disassemble / Process Items. "Add new item" asks for the kind first
   (Service, Inventory part, Non-inventory part, Inventory assembly, Other charge,
   and on sales also Subtotal, Group, Discount, Payment); a purchase line offers only
   what can be bought, a processing output only inventory parts and assemblies. The
   new item's description, price or cost and tax code fill in on the line.
 - Who sees it: only users with the Create permission on the Customer Center
   (customers), the Vendor Center (suppliers), the Chart of Accounts plus the special
   permission to change the Chart of Accounts (accounts), or the Item List (items).
   The form in the window checks the same permissions again when it is saved, and the
   new record is in the audit trail as when it is made on its own page.

2. Inventory Center: one "New item" button with every kind of item (Service,
   Inventory part, Non-inventory part, Inventory assembly, Other charge, Subtotal,
   Group, Discount, Payment), as on the Item List. It replaces "New inventory item"
   and "New assembly".

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v37.docx) are updated: Getting Started (Adding a
new customer, supplier, account or item from a drop-down) and Inventory (Inventory
Center).
