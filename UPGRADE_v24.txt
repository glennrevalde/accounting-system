G-SMaRT Accounting - version 24 (2026-10-15)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 23.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_10_15_withholding_bir_advances.sql

It adds the BIR Reports module (Withholding Tax Table, Print BIR Form 2307 and eight
BIR reports), the tables withholding_taxes (seeded with WT on Goods 1%, WT on
Services 2% and WT on VAT 5%) and payment_withholdings, an empty column wht_ids on
vendors and customers, the accounts 1315 Creditable VAT Withheld, 1410 Advances to
Suppliers and 2115 VAT Withheld Payable (only when those codes are free), four
module default accounts (Withholding Tax Payable (Expanded) = 2110, VAT Withheld
Payable = 2115, Creditable VAT Withheld = 1315, Advances to Suppliers = 1410), and
Vendors > Advances to Suppliers with its report. The new pages are given to the
roles that may open Terms, Tax Codes & Other Lists (the table), the Unpaid Bills
Detail (Form 2307 and the reports) and Pay Bills (advances). Nothing is deleted and
no amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several companies,
run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update
all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
and database/schema.sql include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a
live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/wht.js and assets/css/app.css.

The user's manual has a new chapter 10 (BIR Reports), so the chapters after it are
renumbered. The old files views/manual/chapters/10-administration.html,
11-company-owner.html, 12-super-admin.html and 13-appendices.html are no longer used
and may be deleted.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v24.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Edit Receive Items: an item receipt (bill to follow) has an Edit button. Its date,
    delivery slip number, site, memo and lines can be changed; stock, Inventory and
    Accounts Payable are posted again and the purchase order it was received against
    shows the new quantities received. A receipt whose bill has been entered is
    changed as the bill. To change the vendor, void the receipt and receive again.
 2. Edit Inventory Transfer: date, From and To sites, memo and lines. The stock moves
    again at average cost; "On hand at source" counts what the transfer itself took.
 3. Edit Inventory Adjustment: the adjustment opens on lines (Per Item). "Qty on hand"
    and "Current value" are what the site had without it, so the new quantity, the
    difference and the new value read as they did when it was entered. Saving takes
    it off the books and posts it again in one step; the reason for the adjustment
    and the reason for the change are both kept.
 4. Edit Build Assemblies: assembly, site, quantity, date and memo. The components
    follow the quantity (as recorded on the build, or the bill of materials when
    another assembly is chosen). A pending build stays pending unless "Save and
    complete the build" is chosen.
    All four edits need the Edit right on their page, refuse voided documents and the
    closed period, check the stock (the negative-stock question is asked as on new
    documents) and keep the before and after with the reason in the audit trail.
 5. Withholding Tax Table (BIR Reports > Setup): the guide to how much is withheld,
    flexible: add, change, deactivate or delete (when unused) a tax with its code,
    BIR ATC, name, kind (income tax withheld, or VAT withheld), rate, base (net of
    VAT, as the guide says, or gross), whom it applies to (payments to suppliers,
    from customers, or both) and its accounts. Seeded: WT on Goods 1% (WC158), WT on
    Services 2% (WC160), WT on VAT 5% (WV010). The same page holds "This company is
    a withholding agent" (when it is off, Pay Bills has no Withholding column) and
    the payor zip code and authorized signatory printed on Form 2307.
 6. Withholding tax of suppliers: the vendor form has a Withholding taxes box (one,
    two or more taxes, e.g. 1% and 5%); the Vendor Center shows them and the vendor
    list export has the column. Customers have the same box for the taxes they
    withhold (e.g. a government buyer: 5% VAT and 1%).
 7. Pay Bills: a Withholding column. Clicking it opens the Withholding Tax Table with
    a check box per tax: tick one or more per bill (the supplier's usual taxes are
    ticked when its bill is chosen). Each amount is worked out at once from the
    outstanding balance net of VAT (open balance x subtotal / total), and can be
    typed over, as can the base. The amount to pay is the open balance less the
    discount, the credit, the advance used and the taxes withheld. Posting: Dr
    Accounts Payable (what the bill is settled by), Cr Bank (cash paid), Cr
    Withholding Tax Payable (1% / 2%), Cr VAT Withheld Payable (5%), as in the guide.
    Edit Bill Payment has the same column. The bill payment shows the taxes withheld
    and a BIR Form 2307 button.
 8. Receive Payments: the Withholding column uses the same list for the taxes the
    customer withheld; 1% / 2% go to Creditable Withholding Tax, 5% to Creditable VAT
    Withheld (never Input Tax). Payments saved before keep their withholding.
 9. Print BIR Form 2307 (BIR Reports): choose the supplier, year and quarter. The
    certificate follows the official layout (January 2018 ENCS): period, payee and
    payor TIN, name, address and zip, Part III with the income payments subject to
    expanded withholding tax by ATC for the 1st, 2nd and 3rd month of the quarter,
    the total and the tax withheld, then the money payments subject to withholding
    of business tax (VAT withheld), the declaration and the signature blocks. The
    list on the left shows every supplier with taxes withheld in the quarter and
    whether its 2307 was printed; Print all prints one certificate per supplier.
    Each print is recorded in the audit trail.
10. BIR Reports (a new report group and module), following the attached guide
    ("VAT, Withholding Tax and Company Tax Returns"): Summary of Taxes Withheld from
    Vendors/Suppliers; Expanded Withholding Tax Register (1% / 2%, Form E, for
    suppliers or customers, with the 2307 status and the return 0619-E / 1601-EQ);
    Withholding Tax Remittances (0619-E months 1-2, 1601-EQ quarterly with the 0619-E
    credit, 1600-VT monthly, with the due dates); VAT Withholding Register (1600-VT,
    Form F); Output VAT / Sales Register (Form B: net taxable sales, output VAT 12%,
    invoice total, income WHT 1% / 2%, VAT WHT 5%, actual cash received); Input VAT /
    Purchases Register (Form C); Creditable VAT Withheld Schedule (Form D, 2550Q
    Schedule 3); Quarterly VAT Return Worksheet (Form A with the 2550Q items 31A,
    31B, 37B, 44A, 44B, 50B, 51B, 60B, 61, 15, 16, 20 and 21, the input VAT carried
    over, and the income tax withheld shown apart as not VAT). Each carries the
    guide's notes and "Internal worksheet only, not a substitute for BIR filing
    forms", with Prepared by / Reviewed by / Date lines. "Last quarter" is a new
    date choice on every report.
11. Advance Payment to Supplier (Vendors > Advances to Suppliers): record money paid
    to a supplier before the goods or services come (supplier, date, amount, paid
    from, check / reference, advance account, expected date, what it pays for).
    Posting: Dr Advances to Suppliers, Cr Bank. When the bill comes, Pay Bills shows
    "Advances avail." and "Use advance" for the supplier's bills: the advance applied
    is posted Dr Accounts Payable, Cr Advances to Suppliers (number AAP-), and the
    taxes withheld are deducted when the advance is applied. An advance can be edited
    (not below what was applied) and is voided after its applications. Report:
    Vendors > Reports > Advances to Suppliers (what is left to apply, by supplier,
    with the general-ledger balance). The Vendor Center has an Advance payment button
    and shows the advances left; the Check Register lists advances paid by check.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v24.docx) are updated for all of the above.
