G-SMaRT Accounting - version 26 (2026-10-29)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 25.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_10_29_pos_ending_inventory_shift_times.sql

It adds the report page Ending Inventory - POS Cashier (Point of Sale > Reports),
given to the roles that may open the X- and Z-Reading Summary (with their print
right) and to the roles that may open Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings (the cashiers,
who print their own). No table or column is added, nothing is deleted and no amount
changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several companies, run it on
every company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update all
companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v26.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sheet 2
 1. Date-and-time filters on the Inventory Movement Summary Report, the Inventory
    Movement Detailed Report and the Sales Report Through Inventory Movement (and the
    Inventory Movement Transactions they open): a time after the From date and the
    To date. A business open 24 hours with three shifts (12:01 AM - 8:00 AM,
    8:01 AM - 2:00 PM, 2:01 PM - 12:00 AM) sees the beginning inventory, the sales,
    purchases, other in and out transactions and the ending inventory of each shift.
    The From time counts from the start of its minute and the To time to the end of
    its minute, so the shifts follow each other without a gap; empty times mean whole
    days. A transaction counts on its date at the time of day it was recorded (a POS
    sale at the moment it was rung up). The ending inventory of one shift is the
    beginning inventory of the next, and the three shifts add up to the day.
 2. Cashier filter on the Inventory Movement Summary and Detailed Reports (the
    Sales report already had it): the site becomes the outlet the cashier worked at
    in the period, the stock the cashier is answerable for. A Shift filter on all
    three reports sets the dates and times to the moment a POS shift was opened and
    closed, with its cashier and site.
 3. Sales Report Through Inventory Movement: "Exclude zero sales" leaves out the
    items with no sales in the date range (of the cashier, when one is chosen).
Sheet 1
 4. Ending Inventory - POS Cashier (Point of Sale > Reports): the quantity on hand of
    every item at a cashier's outlet at the cut-off. Filters: Dates with From and To
    date and time, Item, Site, Class, Category, Cashier, Shift (places the time the
    cashier opened and closed the shift automatically), Report type and "Exclude
    inventory with zero balance". Report type A: Item code and Total, in item-code
    order; report type B: Description and Total, in description order. TOTAL line;
    signature lines "Inventory Turnovered by:" and "Inventory Received by:" whose
    captions, names and foot text can be changed in Modify Report (customizable
    footer). "Print to POS printer" prints the slip (QUANTITY ON HAND BY CASHIER,
    By Item Code / By Item Description, Cut-off: To:, Site:, Cashier:) sized to the
    58 mm or 80 mm roll, with A or B chosen on the print page, and through the
    Bluetooth printer when it is switched on. A cashier sees only their own shifts and
    the report opens on their latest shift. Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings has an
    Ending inventory button on each shift.

The demo data now records yesterday's POS sales at times within the demo shift.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v26.docx) are updated for all of the above.
