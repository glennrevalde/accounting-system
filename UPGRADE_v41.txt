G-SMaRT Accounting - version 41 (2027-02-04)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 40.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
1. Back up the database.
2. Run database/migrations/2027_02_04_community.sql on it (phpMyAdmin: Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File). It works on MySQL 5.5 and can be run a second
   time. It adds:
    - the Community tables: channels (and the roles of a restricted channel),
      channel members and how far each has read, messages, reactions, files,
      inquiry categories, inquiries, responses, private conversations and their
      messages, blocked users, community settings, notifications, reports,
      mutes and suspensions, and the moderation log;
    - six channels (General Discussion, Questions and Inquiries, Help and
      Support, Announcements, Suggestions and Feedback, Community Concerns) and
      six inquiry categories;
    - the menu Community with Community Chat and Questions & Inquiries for every
      role (roles that may create anything anywhere may post; the others read),
      and Community Management for the roles that may open User Accounts (the
      Administrator always has it).
   Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. With several companies, run it on
   every company's database (Super Admin: Companies > Update databases).
3. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the page script and the style sheet changed).

What is new: the Community
--------------------------
1. Community Chat (Community > Community Chat)
 - Channels on the left, messages in the middle with the box to write in,
   pinned messages and the people active now on the right; on a phone the two
   side parts slide in.
 - Messages appear without reloading the page. Unread messages are counted per
   channel and per private conversation.
 - Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line; @ mentions (with a list of
   names), emoji, pictures and documents (up to 6 per message: JPG, PNG, GIF,
   WebP, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text, ZIP; up to 10 MB each, or
   the PHP limit), drop or paste a screenshot, links made clickable.
 - On each message: React, Reply, Edit and Delete (own), Copy link, Report;
   moderators: Pin, Hide, Delete.
 - Search a channel by words, with files, pinned, or mentioning me.
 - Sending..., Sent, Not sent (Retry / Discard), and Seen in private messages.
   A message sent twice on a bad connection is kept once.
 - Private messages between two people: only those two can read them, no
   administrator or moderator can open them. Each person chooses who may
   message them (everyone, moderators only, nobody) and can block and report.
 - Settings: picture, who may send private messages, which notifications.
 - Announcements: only moderators post; everyone is notified.

2. Questions & Inquiries (Community > Questions & Inquiries)
 - Ask a question with a title, category, description and attachments;
   similar questions are shown while the title is typed.
 - Responses (with files), comments on responses, Official responses from
   moderators, and the accepted answer chosen by the author or a moderator.
 - Status: Open, Answered (an answer accepted), Resolved (closed; Reopen).
 - List with tabs by status, search (questions and answers), category, sort,
   Mine and No responses yet.

3. Notifications
 - The chat icon in the top bar shows the unread community notifications:
   mentions, replies, private messages, responses, official responses,
   accepted answers, resolved inquiries and announcements. A click opens the
   conversation or the inquiry. One notification per conversation or inquiry,
   never duplicated.

4. Community Management (moderators)
 - Overview: activity, unresolved inquiries, open reports; publish an
   announcement (pinned if ticked).
 - Reports: the words reported, the reason; hide the post, close or dismiss.
   A reported private message is seen only as the words reported.
 - Channels: add, edit, reorder, archive and restore; limit a channel to some
   roles. Inquiry categories: add, rename, stop using.
 - Members: mute (may read, not post) or suspend (no community), for 1 hour to
   30 days or until lifted, with the reason the member sees.
 - Moderation log; every moderation is also in the Audit Trail.
 - Rights: Community Management View = moderator; Edit = hide, pin, announce,
   official answers, reports; Delete = delete others' posts, mute, suspend;
   Create = channels and categories.

5. Safety
 - Every action is checked on the server; text is always shown as text; posts
   are rate-limited (too many too fast, or the same text repeated, is refused);
   files are checked and kept in the database; the page's background requests
   do not keep an idle session alive (the automatic log-out still applies).

The user's manual has a new chapter 14, Community (the Appendices are now
chapter 15), in the Help pages and in the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v41.docx).
