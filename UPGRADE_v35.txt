G-SMaRT Accounting - version 35 (2026-12-24)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 34.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
1. Back up the database.
2. Run database/migrations/2026_12_24_fb_pos.sql on it (phpMyAdmin: Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File). It works on MySQL 5.5 and can be run a second time.
   It adds:
    - four columns on items: fb_pos (shown on the FB POS, 0 at first),
      fb_group_id (its menu group), fb_sort (its order) and image_file (its picture);
    - the table fb_pos_groups (the menu groups, empty at first);
    - the pages Point of Sale > FB POS, for the roles that may open the POS
      Register, and Point of Sale > FB POS Menu & Groups, for the roles that may
      open POS Terminals, with the same rights.
   Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. Until it is run, the system stops
   after log-in with a page that names it.
3. Log in as an administrator and run System Administration > Utilities: Verify &
   Rebuild Data > Rebuild data once. It works the costs of all the inventory
   transactions out again in date order, so the averages that jumped after
   processing orders, back-dated bills and edits are put right (see below).
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

The item pictures are saved in storage/items (storage/companies/cNNN/items for
the other companies). The web server must be able to write in the storage folder,
as it already must for backups and the company logo.

What is new
-----------
1. Disassemble / Process Items: outputs must cost what the inputs cost
 - A processing order is not saved when the total Allocated cost of its outputs
   differs by more than 2% from the cost of its inputs plus labor and other costs:
   "The outputs are costed at 156,565.90, but the inputs cost 15,581.25 (...): a
   difference of ... The outputs may differ from the input cost by at most 2%.
   Revise the Allocated cost or Unit cost of the outputs (or their Basis) so they
   add up to the input cost."
 - The form shows the difference and its percentage under the outputs while you
   type, and a red warning as soon as it is beyond 2%, before you save. Save and
   post, Save as pending and Complete and post are all checked.
 - The 2% is a setting: System Administration > Company, Security & Closing Date >
   Inventory and POS > "Processing orders: largest difference allowed ...".
 - The cause of the case reported: under "Current average cost" the Basis of an
   output is a cost PER UNIT. A total typed there (or a formula whose basis is a
   total) costed every unit at that total, ten times the input cost, and the
   difference was posted to the variance account. Orders saved before this version
   are not changed: the Average Cost Changes report (Inventory > Reports, tick
   "Processing, disassembly and builds only") shows them; open each and correct
   its Allocated cost.

2. Average cost: the later transactions are costed again
 - The average unit cost is worked out as each transaction is posted. A bill,
   processing order, adjustment, transfer or sale that was back-dated, or edited,
   voided or deleted after later ones were posted, left the later ones at the
   costs of the moment they were entered. That is what made the averages jump
   (263.64 > 110.45 > 260.56 > 568.78; 514.52 > 112.65): a processing order
   costed its outputs at ten times their value, the stock was sold at the inflated
   average, then the order was corrected and the few units left carried the rest.
 - Now, after such a save, void or delete, every inventory transaction dated from
   that date is posted again in date order (on each date the stock received before
   the stock used or sold), exactly as Rebuild data does for the whole history. The
   averages, the cost of sales and their journal entries follow the dates. A blue
   message says how many were costed again; the audit trail records it (recost).
 - A new transaction dated today (a POS sale, a bill) has nothing after it and
   starts nothing, so the counter is not slowed down.
 - Two settings under Inventory and POS: switch it off, and the largest number of
   transactions costed again at once (2,000; above that nothing is changed and a
   message asks you to run Rebuild data).

3. FB POS (Point of Sale > FB POS)
 - A second selling screen for a food and beverage counter: the items are shown as
   pictures with their name, price and stock, under menu groups shown as tabs
   (All, the groups in your order, Other). Tap a picture to add it; + and - change
   the quantity; a search box and barcode scanning work too.
 - It sells exactly as the POS Register does: the same terminals, shifts, cash
   drops and readings, payment methods, approval codes, suspend / recall, charge to
   account, counter payments, receipts, returns and voids, and the same Point of
   Sale reports. Only the screen differs.
 - Point of Sale > FB POS Menu & Groups: add, rename, order, hide and delete the
   menu groups; tick which items are on the FB POS, choose their group and order,
   and save the whole menu at once.
 - Item List > the item > FB POS: the picture (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, up to 3 MB),
   Show on the FB POS, Menu group and Order. Inventory parts, assemblies,
   non-inventory parts, services, other charges and item groups can be shown.
 - On a phone the order moves under the menu and a bar at the bottom shows the
   number of items and the total.

Setting up the FB POS
 1. Run the migration (above).
 2. Point of Sale > FB POS Menu & Groups: add the groups, for example Combo Meals,
    Burgers, Fries & Sides and Drinks.
 3. On the same page choose Show: All items that can be, tick the items, choose
    their group and click Save the menu.
 4. Open each item and upload its picture under FB POS.
 5. A cashier with an open shift opens Point of Sale > FB POS.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v35.docx) are updated: Point of Sale (FB POS, FB
POS Menu & Groups), Inventory (the FB POS part of the item form, the 2% rule,
costing again) and System Administration (the three new settings).
