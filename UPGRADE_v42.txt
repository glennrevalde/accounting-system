G-SMaRT Accounting - version 42 (2027-02-11)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 41.1.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
1. Back up the database.
2. Run database/migrations/2027_02_11_voucher_signatures.sql on it (phpMyAdmin:
   Import; Navicat: Execute SQL File). It works on MySQL 5.5 and can be run a
   second time. It adds four empty tables:
    - voucher_sign_requests     the signature links (only a fingerprint of the
                                secret part of each link is used to find it)
    - voucher_signatures        the signatures, who signed which line and when
    - voucher_documents and
      voucher_document_chunks   the PDF copies: the original unsigned voucher
                                and every signed copy
   Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. With several companies, run it on
   every company's database (Super Admin: Companies > Update databases).
3. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the style sheet changed and there is a
   new script, assets/js/signature.js).
4. The storage folder must stay writable by the web server: the key of the
   signature links is created in storage/keys (storage/companies/cNNN/keys for
   the other companies) the first time a link is made.

What is new: Digital Voucher Signatures
---------------------------------------
For the Check Voucher (Write Checks), the Cash Voucher and the Journal Entry.
The signature lines are those of the printed voucher: Prepared by, Checked by,
Approved by and, on checks and cash vouchers, Received by.

Sign Voucher (a user of the system)
 - Open the voucher and click Sign Voucher (title bar or Signatures box).
 - Review the voucher: Page (the printed voucher, fitted to the screen) or PDF;
   Open PDF opens it in a new tab.
 - Choose the signature line, draw the signature with the mouse, a finger or a
   stylus (Undo, Redo, Clear; on a phone a larger pad), tick "I reviewed ... and I
   sign it as shown", click Confirm and sign, and confirm once more.

Request Signature (another person, no account needed)
 - In the Signatures box: Request Signature, type the signer's name (and email
   if you like), choose the line, the expiry (1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days) and a
   message, click Generate link.
 - Copy Link (or Email the link / Share) and send it to that person only.
 - The person opens the link (sign.php), reads the whole voucher, signs, ticks
   that they reviewed it and agree to sign, clicks Submit Signature and
   confirms. Cancel leaves without signing. Afterwards the page thanks them and
   offers the signed copy for one hour.

The same in both ways
 - The signature is drawn on its line of the voucher PDF, with the signer's
   name and the date, and a new signed PDF is saved (version 1, 2, ...). It ends
   with a signature record: line, signer, method, date and time, IP address,
   and the fingerprint (SHA-256) of the voucher content that was signed.
 - The original unsigned voucher is kept as a PDF at the first signature.
 - Printing the voucher shows the signatures on the lines.

The Signatures box on the voucher
 - Status: Unsigned, Awaiting signature, Partially signed (2 of 4), Fully
   signed; also shown as "Signatures" in the voucher details.
 - Each line: the signature, the signer, the date and time, and "in the system"
   or "through a signature link"; a line waiting for a link shows to whom it
   was sent, when it expires, whether it was opened, Copy link and Revoke.
 - The signed PDF (preview, download), earlier signed copies, the original
   unsigned voucher, and the signature activity.

Security and rules
 - A link holds a 256-bit random secret and no voucher number: changing the
   address cannot reach another voucher. The database keeps only its SHA-256
   fingerprint to find it (and the link encrypted, so it can be copied again).
 - A link is for one person and one line, works once, expires, and can be
   revoked; an expired, revoked, used or altered link says so and signs nothing.
   Unknown links answer slowly. The page is not cached, not indexed, and sends
   no address to other sites.
 - The voucher content the signer reviewed is checked again when the signature
   is submitted; if it changed, the signature is refused.
 - One signature per line; each user signs one line of a voucher; a second
   submit of the same signature is refused.
 - A signed voucher cannot be edited, voided or deleted. A user with the
   Delete/Void right on its page clicks Reset signatures and gives a reason;
   the signatures are taken off (kept on record with the reason) and the
   voucher can be corrected and signed again.
 - Rights follow the voucher's page (Write Checks, Cash Voucher, Make Journal
   Entries): Edit or Approve to sign and to send or revoke links, Delete/Void
   to reset, View to see and download the PDF copies.
 - Audit trail: Sign Request, Sign Link Open, Sign, Sign Revoke, Sign Reset and
   Sign Pdf, under the voucher. An outside signer is shown as "Signer: name".

Optional setting: the address of the links
 - A link starts with the address the program was opened with. If outside
   people will sign, open the program through its public address before
   making links, or add this line inside the 'app' settings of
   config/config.php:
       'public_url' => 'https://accounts.example.com/accounting-system',
