G-SMaRT Accounting - version 33 (2026-12-10)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 32.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run database/migrations/2026_12_10_class_reports.sql once on every company database
(phpMyAdmin > Import, Navicat > Execute SQL File, or the mysql command). It is
written for MySQL 5.5 and is safe to run twice. Until it is run, the program stops
after log-in and names it. (If 2026_12_03_settle_receipts_financial_menu.sql of
version 32 was not run yet, run it first.) It adds the two new report pages and gives
them to the roles that may open the Trial Balance and the Statement of Cash Flows,
with their print right. No table or column is added and nothing is changed.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v33.xlsx")
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Point of Sale > Reports > POS Sales by Hour: the TOTAL row adds up the
    Transactions column too, and its Average sale is the total net sales divided by
    all the transactions.
 2. Inventory > Reports > Physical Inventory Worksheet: a new tick box, Exclude zero
    qty. The items with no quantity on hand at the site (or at all sites) on the As of
    date are left off the sheet, and the note under the title says so. It works with
    a blind count as well.
 3. Financial > Reports > Trial Balance by Class (new). For each account, a Debit and
    a Credit column for every class (the active classes and any class with activity,
    then Unclassified), then Total Debit and Total Credit, which are the Trial Balance
    of the whole company. Income and expense accounts show the fiscal year to date and
    earlier years' net income is in Retained Earnings, class by class, as on the
    Trial Balance. "One column per class (debit less credit)" shows a single net column
    per class instead. A click on an amount opens the General Ledger of that account
    for that class. The note names any class whose own debits and credits differ.
 4. Financial > Reports > Statement of Cash Flows by Class (new). The Statement of
    Cash Flows (indirect method) with one column per class and a Total, which is the
    plain statement: operating, investing and financing activities, the net cash
    increase, and the cash at the beginning and end of the period, class by class.
    The Statement of Cash Flows itself now uses the same code and gives the same
    figures as before.
 5. The Class List has two more buttons: Trial Balance by Class and Cash Flows by
    Class. Both reports are also in the Financial Reports card of the Report Center.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v33.docx) are updated for all of the above.
