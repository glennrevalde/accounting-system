G-SMaRT Accounting - version 23 (2026-10-08)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 22.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_10_08_custom_report_deposit_edit.sql

It adds the Customized Inventory Report page (for the roles that may open the
Inventory Valuation Summary) and one empty column, transaction_lines.pos_shift_id,
which links a deposit line to the POS shift whose cash it deposits; the deposits
already made are linked by the "(shift #n)" words on their lines. Nothing is deleted
and no amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several
companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database
updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
and database/schema.sql include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a
live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/app.js and assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v23.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Item form: an inventory part or assembly that still has a balance (stock at any
    site, or a value) cannot be made inactive. The Active box stays ticked and
    locked, with the reason; saving it inactive anyway, or an import with
    is_active = No, is refused. Items with no balance, and services and the other
    types, can be made inactive as before.
 2. Item List: the search is incremental. Typing a part of a code, name, barcode,
    category or supplier narrows the list at once, word by word, with a count of the
    matches; Enter opens the first item. The category and Include inactive boxes
    apply at once (no Filter button). Export takes the words typed.
 3. Customized Inventory Report (Inventory > Reports): one line per item with the
    columns the user picks in its Columns box (open to everybody who may open the
    report): item details (descriptions, barcode, category, type, unit, supplier, tax
    code, status, reorder point, maximum stock), stock and value at the end of the
    period (on hand, below reorder point, average cost, asset value and %, sales price,
    retail value and %, gross profit in stock), profit per unit (purchase cost, gross
    profit per unit, gross profit % per unit, markup %), sales of the period (qty sold,
    sales amount, average selling price, cost of sales, GROSS PROFIT, GROSS PROFIT %,
    % of sales, last sold) and purchases of the period (qty bought, amount, last
    bought). Filters: dates, item, site, category, item type, supplier, which items.
    It prints, exports, memorizes, and can be redesigned with Modify Report.
    Every report about items (Inventory Valuation Summary among them) can also add
    the item fields gross profit per unit, gross profit %, markup %, asset value,
    retail value, purchase description, tax code, maximum stock and status through
    Modify Report.
 4. Edit Make Deposit: a deposit has an Edit button (Edit right on Make Deposits).
    Edit Deposit shows what it holds ticked: take payments or POS shifts off (they wait
    to be deposited again) or add others, change the date, account, slip number, memo,
    class, other receipts and cash back. The deposit is posted again, with the change
    and its reason in the audit trail.
 5. Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings: a date filter on the list of shifts (Today,
    Yesterday, This/Last week, This/Last month or custom From/To), with Status,
    Terminal and Cashier, totals for the period and pages of 100 shifts, so earlier
    days can be seen. Without dates the latest 30 shifts show, as before.
 6. Corrected shifts and Make Deposits: the cash to deposit of a shift is the cash
    counted less the opening float (it stays in the drawer) plus the cash drops, from
    the corrected figures. In your example (float 3,000.00, counted corrected from
    9,170.48 to 12,170.48) Make Deposits proposes 9,170.48, which was 6,170.48 before
    the correction; the page now explains the calculation and marks corrected shifts.
    A shift already deposited can now be corrected too: the deposit follows on its
    own (its line for the shift, its total and its ledger entry), and the message and
    the shift's corrections list say so. The shift page shows the cash to deposit,
    also while the correction is typed.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v23.docx) are updated for all of the above.
