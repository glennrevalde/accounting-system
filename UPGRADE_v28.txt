G-SMaRT Accounting - version 28 (2026-11-12)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 27.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
No database update this time: there is no new migration, and no table or column
changes. database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql are
unchanged. (If the 2026_11_05 migration of version 27 was not run yet, run it.)

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/css/app.css.

What is new or corrected (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v28.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Make Deposits: card and e-wallet settlements
 1. E-wallet Clearing on Make Deposits did not agree with the General Ledger:
    it showed twice its balance (74,670.70 against 37,335.35 on the Trial
    Balance). The cause: the list of clearing accounts was joined to the payment
    methods, and E-wallet Clearing is the clearing account of two methods (GCash
    and Maya), so every ledger line of the account was counted once per method.
    Corrected: each clearing account is listed once, with the payment methods that
    use it under its name, and Unsettled is the balance of the account in the
    General Ledger. Only the figure shown was wrong; the books were not affected.
 2. The balances, checked and corrected for Card Clearing and E-wallet Clearing:
    - Unsettled is the General Ledger balance on the deposit Date (the same
      figure the Trial Balance shows for that date). Changing the Date updates it,
      and a settlement is checked against the balance on the deposit date: a
      deposit dated before the sales cannot settle them.
    - The module default accounts Card Clearing and E-wallet Clearing are always
      listed: a card or e-wallet method with no clearing account of its own posts
      its sales there, and before they were left off the list.
    - Every clearing account is shown, also with a zero balance; a credit balance
      (more settled than received) is shown in red.
    - A payment on account taken at the POS register with a card or e-wallet
      method that has no clearing account went to Undeposited Funds, while a POS
      sale paid the same way went to the clearing account. It now goes to Card
      Clearing / E-wallet Clearing too, like the sale. Payments already saved are
      not changed.
    - A fee with no gross amount, and a fee larger than the gross, are refused
      with a message.
 3. A Details button beside each unsettled balance opens the card and e-wallet
    balance details (also as a page that can be printed, with another account or
    as-of date): the balance with its payment methods; what it is made of by
    source (POS sales, POS returns refunded, customer payments, sales receipts,
    settlements deposited, journal entries) with documents, debits, credits and
    net; the card and e-wallet sales by payment method; the receipts not yet
    settled (the latest receipts that make up the balance, settlements being taken
    against the oldest receipts first, each with its date, document, customer or
    terminal, method, reference, amount and unsettled part); and the latest 25
    settlements. Links open the General Ledger and the Trial Balance of the
    account.

Processing Order
 4. The unit cost of each input on the printed PROCESSING ORDER (Item,
    Description, Qty, Unit cost, Cost).
 5. The variance quantity, Input Quantity less Output Quantity, on the printout
    ("Variance qty (inputs less outputs)", under the cost variance; also printed
    for users who may not see costs), on the saved order (a line under Total
    outputs and a Quantity summary box), live on the entry form (a Total inputs row
    and a Variance qty row under the outputs), as a column of the Processing
    Orders list and its CSV export, and on the Processing & Disassembly Register
    (Variance qty; Input qty and Output qty can be added in Modify Report). For
    example 5.02 kg of pork cut into 2.3 kg and 2.7 kg: variance qty 0.02.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v28.docx) are updated for all of the above.
