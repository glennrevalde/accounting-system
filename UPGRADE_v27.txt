G-SMaRT Accounting - version 27 (2026-11-05)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 26.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_11_05_item_groups_receiving_lists.sql

It adds an empty column group_variance_account_id on items, five empty group
columns on transaction_lines (group_item_id, group_ref, group_qty, group_rate,
group_role), the table item_group_sales, the account "Item Group Price Variance"
(Income, code 4070 or the next free code) with its module default account, and an
empty column received_via on transactions, filled in for the item receipts already
saved. It adds the report pages Sales by Item Group (Customers > Sales Reports,
for the roles that may open Sales by Item Summary) and POS Sales by Item Group
(Point of Sale > Reports, for the roles that may open POS Sales by Item), each with
its print right. Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5,
safe to run twice. With several companies, run it on every company database (Super
Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the
program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v27.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Item groups (Inventory > Item List, type Group)
 1. Group price: the price followed on Estimates, Sales Orders, Sales Receipts,
    Invoices and POS sales when the group is chosen (with the customer's price
    level applied, like any item).
 2. Consolidated price: the sum of the prices of the items in the group (each
    item's price times its quantity in the group), recalculated as items are
    added, removed or their quantity changed.
 3. Price variance (Group price - Consolidated price), shown next to them.
 4. Price variance GL account: the account the variance is posted to. Left blank,
    the module default "Item Group Price Variance" is used (Chart of Accounts >
    Module Default Accounts).
 5. The items table of the group shows the Price and the Amount of each item, with
    the sum of the individual prices (the consolidated price) at the foot.
 6. A "Sold as a group" card: the groups sold this month and this fiscal year and
    what they sold for, with a link to Sales by Item Group.

Settings (System Administration > Company, Security & Closing Date > Inventory and POS)
 7. "Track Item Group Sales transactions": when ticked, every group sold is
    recorded in its own table (item_group_sales) and appears on Sales by Item Group
    and POS Sales by Item Group. When not ticked, nothing new is recorded there.
 8. "Adopt the Group Price in the Item Group": when ticked, the Group price is
    followed on sales; when not ticked (or the group has no Group price), the
    consolidated price (the items' own prices) is followed.
    Both are ticked as delivered.

How a group is saved
 9. A group is entered as one line. On saving, the document keeps the items of the
    group at their own prices (so stock, cost of goods sold and the item reports
    stay correct) plus one price variance line on the Price variance GL account for
    the difference, so the document total equals the group price. A discount on the
    group line is carried by the variance line. All these lines remember the group,
    its quantity and its price.
10. Viewing or editing a saved Estimate, Sales Order, Sales Receipt, Invoice or POS
    sale that has groups offers two buttons: "View by item group" (one line per
    group, as entered; the default) and "View item details" (each item of the group
    and the price variance line). Both can be edited and saved; the print follows
    the view chosen. Groups carry over when an Estimate or Sales Order is turned
    into an invoice, in part or in full.

Point of Sale
11. Item groups can be sold in the POS register like on the Customer Invoice:
    search or scan the group, it goes into the cart as one line at its group price.
    The receipt shows the groups with the same two views. Suspended sales, voids and
    returns (whole groups) work with groups.

Reports
12. Sales by Item Group (Customers > Sales Reports): the groups sold in every
    module (invoices, sales receipts, statement charges, POS sales, less credit
    memos and POS returns). Filters: Dates, Customer, Site, Class, Sales rep, Item
    group.
13. POS Sales by Item Group (Point of Sale > Reports): POS sales less POS returns.
    Filters: Dates, Terminal, Site, Cashier, Item group.
    Both read Item Group Name / Qty / Group Price / Total with a Totals line, and
    "Show the individual item details" lists the items of each group (and its price
    variance) under it; "Show the transactions" lists the documents. Consolidated
    price, Price variance, Tax and Transactions can be added in Modify Report.

Lists with date filters
14. Receipt Management: a "Receipt List" button opens the list of receipts with
    Dates (From and To; by receipt date or upload date), Status, Vendor and Search,
    a total and Export to CSV.
15. Receive Items and Enter Bills: a "Receive Items and Enter Bills List" button
    opens the bills entered on that page, with Dates and the usual filters.
16. Receive Items: a "Receive Items List" button opens the item receipts (and the
    bills they became), with Dates and the usual filters.

Notes
 - Documents saved before this version carry no group information: they show and
   report their item lines as before, and are not on the item group reports.
 - Bills entered before this version appear in the Receive Items and Enter Bills
   List only when they can be recognised (memo "Received against ..." written by
   that page); new ones are always recorded.
 - Open each item group once and enter its Group price and, if needed, its Price
   variance GL account. Until a Group price is entered, the consolidated price is
   followed.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v27.docx) are updated for all of the above.
