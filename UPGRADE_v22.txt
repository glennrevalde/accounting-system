G-SMaRT Accounting - version 22 (2026-10-07)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 21.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

New files: app/CompanyAccess.php, modules/auth/switch_company.php,
           modules/vendors/_bill_payment_edit.php

Database
--------
No company database change in this version: there is no migration to run.
With several companies, the platform (master) database gets one new table,
user_company_links, the first time a user of another company is added. The program
creates it by itself (CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS, MySQL 5.5 safe); it is also in
database/master_schema.sql for new installations. Do NOT import
database/accounting_system.sql or accounting_system_clean.sql over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/app.js and assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v22.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Edit bill payments. A bill payment now has an Edit button (users with Edit on
    Pay Bills). Edit Bill Payment changes the date, check / payment number, method,
    payment account, reference, memo, class and print mark, and which of the vendor's
    bills it pays and by how much (with discounts). Saving posts the payment again,
    works out the balance and status of every bill it paid before or pays now, and
    records the change with its reason in the audit trail. Closed periods, bank
    reconciliations and the supervisor approval above the amount limit apply as on
    every transaction. The vendor stays the same (a payment to the wrong vendor is
    voided and entered again).
 2. Negative Inventory Listing: an As of date filter. Today shows the quantities on
    hand now; an earlier date shows the items below zero on that day, from every
    movement up to that date, with the last issue up to that date.
 3. Physical Inventory Worksheet: an As of date filter. The system quantities are
    those of the date chosen (today: on hand now), for one site or all sites.
 4. Adjust Quantity/Value on Hand: the Category selection takes two or more
    categories. Per Batch: "Categories" is a drop-down list of check boxes (Tick all,
    Clear, search, Show); the grid lists the items of every ticked category, and Post
    adjustment & New keeps them. Per Item: "Categories > Add their items" and the
    Categories box of Find & Select Items take several categories too. Old links with
    one category still work.
 5. A user who switches between two companies without being a Super Admin.
    User Accounts (and Employees & Users) > "Add user from another company": type the
    username the person logs in with at their own company and give them roles, sites
    and POS terminals in this company. The person logs in at their own company as
    always; the company name in the top bar becomes a switch between their companies
    (one click, no password), with a blue strip and "Back to ..." while they work in
    the other company. They can do only what the roles of that company allow, their
    password stays with their own company, and supervisor approvals they give use that
    password. Either company can remove the access (User Accounts: "Remove access to
    this company" / "Also works in ... Remove"), and it ends when their own account is
    deactivated; a user working in the other company is then taken back home at the
    next click. Every grant, removal and switch is in the audit trails and the
    platform audit log.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v22.docx) are updated for all of the above.
