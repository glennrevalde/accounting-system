The Community is where the people of the company talk to each other inside G-SMaRT Accounting: chat channels for everyday discussion, help and announcements, private messages between two people, and a place to ask questions and get answers that stay findable. It uses the same log-in and the same users as the rest of the system, and each company has its own community.

It has three pages under Community in the menu: Community Chat, Questions & Inquiries and, for moderators, Community Management. New messages, replies and answers appear by themselves, without reloading the page.

Who may do what

The rights come from Page Access Configuration, like every other page. As installed, every role may read the community; the roles that may create anything anywhere may also post; the roles that may open User Accounts (and the Administrator) are the moderators.

Page and right What it allows
Community Chat, Questions & Inquiries: View Read the channels, the inquiries and the answers.
Create Post messages, react, send private messages, ask questions, respond and comment.
Edit / Delete Change or delete one's own messages, inquiries and responses.
Community Management: View Moderator: sees hidden posts, the dashboard, the reports and the moderation log; is shown as "Moderator" beside their name.

Important

Private messages are seen only by the two people in the conversation. No administrator or moderator can open them. If someone reports a private message, the moderators see only the words that were reported.

Community Chat

Where to find it: Community > Community Chat. The chat icon in the top bar also leads here.

The page has three parts: the channels and private messages on the left, the messages of the open channel in the middle with the box to write in at the bottom, and on the right the channel's description, its pinned messages and the people active now. On a phone the left and right parts slide in with the buttons at the top of the messages.

Channels

Six channels are installed. Moderators can add others, rename them, archive them and limit a channel to some roles (see Community Management).

Channel For
General Discussion Everyday conversations.
Questions and Inquiries Quick questions in chat. For questions that need a lasting answer, use Questions & Inquiries.
Help and Support Help with the system, an account or a technical problem.
Announcements Updates from the administrators. Only moderators post here, and everyone is notified of each announcement.
Suggestions and Feedback Ideas and requests for improvement.
Community Concerns Concerns that can be discussed openly.

A channel with messages you have not read shows their number beside its name, and its name is in bold. The messages are read when you open the channel and scroll to the bottom. A lock beside a name means the channel is open to some roles only.

Writing a message

  • Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter (or click the send button). Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  • @ mentions: type @ and the first letters of a name; choose the person from the list. They are notified.
  • Emoji: the smiling face button opens a set of emoji.
  • Files: the paper clip attaches up to six pictures or documents (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text or ZIP), each up to 10 MB or the server's limit. You can also drop files on the messages or paste a screenshot. Pictures are shown in the message; documents are downloaded with a click.
  • Links typed in a message become clickable.

Under your message, Sending… shows while it is on its way and Sent once it is saved. If the connection is lost, the message stays with Not sent and the buttons Retry and Discard. A message sent twice on a bad connection is kept once. When the connection drops, a line at the top of the messages says so and the page keeps trying.

What you can do with a message

Point at a message (on a phone, tap the three dots beside it) to see its buttons:

  • React with one of eight emoji. Click a reaction under a message to add or take back yours; point at it to see who reacted.
  • Reply: your message shows the one you answer above it; a click on it scrolls to the original. The author is notified.
  • Edit and Delete your own messages (Edit shows "(edited)"; a deleted message shows "This message was deleted."). In the box, the Up arrow edits your last message and Esc cancels.
  • Copy link: a link that opens the channel at this message.
  • Report a message that breaks the rules (see Reports below).
  • Moderators also have Pin to channel, Hide and Delete for any message.

Searching a channel

The magnifier at the top opens the search: type words and choose All messages, With files, Pinned or Mentioning me. A click on a result scrolls to the message (older messages are loaded around it). Load earlier messages at the top of the messages brings back older ones.

Private messages

  1. Click the pencil beside Private messages on the left, or click a name under Active now.
  2. Type a name and click the person. A person who cannot receive your message is listed with the reason (for example, they take private messages from moderators only).
  3. Write as in a channel. Seen shows under your last message once the other person has read it.

The three dots at the top of a conversation offer Block (neither of you can then message the other until you unblock) and Report. Your blocked list is in Settings.

Your community settings

Settings at the top of the page sets your picture (shown beside your messages instead of your initials), who may send you private messages (Everyone in the community, Moderators only or Nobody), which notifications you get, and the people you blocked.

On a phone the chat fills the screen: the button at the top left opens the channels and private messages, and the one at the top right the pinned messages and the people active now.

Community notifications

The chat icon in the top bar, on every page, shows how many community notifications you have not read. Click it to see them; a click on one opens the conversation or the inquiry it is about and marks it read. Mark all as read clears the count.

You are notified when someone mentions you, replies to your message, sends you a private message, responds to or comments on your inquiry, gives an official response to it, accepts your response as the answer, resolves your inquiry, and of every announcement. One notification is kept per conversation or inquiry: a second private message in the same conversation brings the notification up again instead of adding another. Settings turns each kind on or off.

Questions & Inquiries

Where to find it: Community > Questions & Inquiries. Use it for questions that need a proper answer that others can find later: how to do something in the system, a problem with an account or a document, a request.

The list shows every inquiry with its number of responses (green once an answer is accepted), views, status, category, author and last activity. The tabs filter by status; Search looks in the questions and their answers; Category, Sort, Mine and No responses yet narrow it further.

Asking a question

  1. Click Ask a question.
  2. Type the Title. Similar questions already asked appear under it: open them to see whether yours is answered.
  3. Choose the Category and describe the question. Mention people with @username. Attach screenshots or documents if they help.
  4. Click Post inquiry. You are notified of every response.

Responses, comments and the accepted answer

Anyone who may post can respond (with files) or comment on a response. A moderator's response can be posted as an Official response, labelled "Official". The author of the inquiry or a moderator clicks Accept as the answer on the response that solved it: it moves to the top under "Accepted answer" and the inquiry becomes Answered.

Status Meaning
Open Waiting for an answer.
Answered A response was accepted as the answer.
Resolved The matter is closed (Mark as resolved, by the author or a moderator). Reopen opens it again.

You can edit your inquiry and your responses, and delete your responses. An inquiry can be deleted by its author only while it has no responses; after that, mark it resolved. Anything can be reported. When others respond while you are reading, a line at the top offers to show the new responses.

Community Management

Where to find it: Community > Community Management (moderators only). Every action taken here, and every moderation done in the chat or on an inquiry, is written to the moderation log and to the audit trail.

Overview and announcements

The overview shows the messages of today and of the last 7 days, the members active in the last 7 days, the open inquiries, those unanswered for over 2 days, the open reports and the members muted or suspended; the unresolved inquiries (those with no response first); and the activity of each channel.

Publish an announcement posts in the Announcements channel as an official message, pins it if ticked, and notifies everyone who reads the community.

Reports

Members report messages, inquiries, responses, private messages and people, with a reason. Each report shows the words reported as they were when reported, who reported them and why. Hide the post hides it (and closes the other reports of the same post); Close: action taken and Dismiss close the report with an optional note; Mute or suspend opens the member. A reported private message is seen only as the reported words.

Hiding, pinning and deleting

  • Hide (with a reason) keeps a post but shows "This message was hidden by a moderator" to everyone except the moderators and its author; Show again brings it back.
  • Pin to channel lists a message under Pinned on the right of the chat.
  • Delete removes another member's post for good; its words are kept in the moderation log.

Channels and inquiry categories

On Channels: add a channel (name, description, Discussion or Announcements, who posts), edit it, move it up or down, and archive it (it can then be read by moderators but not posted in) or restore it. Only some roles see this channel limits it to the roles ticked; moderators always see it. On Inquiry categories: add, rename, describe and stop using categories.

Muting and suspending members

On Members, find the member and choose Mute (may read, may not post or send private messages except to moderators) or Suspend (no community at all), for 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or until lifted, with the reason, which the member sees. Lift ends it early. Administrators and the Company Owner cannot be muted or suspended.

Moderation log

Every hide, delete, pin, mute, suspension, report handled, announcement and change to channels and categories, with the moderator, the member, the reason and the details. The same actions are in System Administration > Audit Trail, as actions starting with "Community".

Safety and limits

  • Messages are checked before they are saved: sending more than 6 messages in 10 seconds or 30 in a minute, or the same text again and again, is refused for a moment. At most 5 questions an hour and 20 reports a day are taken from one person.
  • Everything you type is shown as text: nobody can put a script or a hidden link in a message.
  • Files are checked when they arrive (pictures must be real pictures, PDF files real PDFs; program files are refused), are kept in the database and are sent only to people who may see what they are attached to.
  • The page asks for new messages every 3 seconds while you look at it (every 15 seconds in a tab in the background). This does not keep you logged in: the automatic log-out after a period of inactivity still applies.
