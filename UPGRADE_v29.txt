G-SMaRT Accounting - version 29 (2026-11-12)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 28.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_11_12_comparative_statements_menu.sql

It renames the menu "Chart of Accounts" to "Financial Set-up & Reports" and adds the
eight comparative report pages, given to the roles that may open the Income Statement
(the four income statements) and the Balance Sheet (the four balance sheets), each
with its print right. No table or column is added, nothing is deleted and no amount
changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several companies, run it on
every company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database updates > Update all
companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v29.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sort by (Increasing / Decreasing)
 1. Income by Customer Summary: Sort by Qty sold, Sales, Cost of sales,
    Income / (loss) or Margin %.
 2. Sales by Item Summary: Sort by Qty, Amount, % of sales, COGS, Gross margin or
    Margin %. The item types (Inventory, Assembly, Service...) and their totals keep
    their places; the items are sorted inside each type.
 3. Inventory Sales by Site: Sort by Qty sold, Sales or Cost of sales, inside each
    site.
 4. Sales by Item Group (and POS Sales by Item Group): Sort by Qty or Total; with the
    item details or the transactions shown, they move with their group.
    The Sort by box and the Increasing / Decreasing box are in the filter bar and
    refresh the report at once; the heading line says "Sorted by Amount, decreasing";
    the column headings of these reports sort the same way. Lines without a figure
    (no cost on a service item) come last. Cost columns are offered only to users
    who may view costs. Default order puts the report back in its own order. A
    memorized report keeps its sort.

Quantity on Hand by Site
 5. "Exclude zero qty": leaves out the items with nothing on hand at the sites
    shown (one site chosen: what that site does not hold).

Financial Set-up & Reports
 6. The menu "Chart of Accounts" is now "Financial Set-up & Reports" (its pages keep
    their names; Chart of Accounts is still the first list in it), and on the
    Report Center its card is "Financial Reports". The Report Center also has a
    "Find a report by name" box and a Memorized Reports button.
 7. Eight new reports in Financial Set-up & Reports > Reports (and the Financial
    Reports card):
      Comparative Income Statement by Month        one column per month
      Comparative Income Statement by Quarter      one column per quarter
      Comparative Income Statement by Year         one column per fiscal year
      Comparative Income Statement by Custom Range up to 4 ranges of your own
      Comparative Balance Sheet by Month           at the end of each month
      Comparative Balance Sheet by Quarter         at the end of each quarter
      Comparative Balance Sheet by Year            at the end of each fiscal year
      Comparative Balance Sheet by Custom Date     as of up to 4 dates of your own
    Filters: Dates / From / To (or the ranges / dates) and Class. The income
    statements have a Total column (not the custom one: its ranges may overlap). The
    last two columns are the change from the period before (first range or date less
    the second on the custom reports) and the % change. Each column agrees with the
    Income Statement or the Balance Sheet run for that period or date, and every
    account figure opens its General Ledger for that column. The year reports open on
    the last three fiscal years; the custom ones on this month beside the same month
    last year, and on today beside the end of the last fiscal year.

Unit costs
 8. Sales of an Inventory Part (or assembly), or of an Item Group with such an item
    in it, are refused when the item has neither a Unit cost (Item List) nor an
    average unit cost: invoices, sales receipts, statement charges and POS sales. The
    message names the items and their group. An item that has a unit cost but no
    stock received yet now goes out at that unit cost (it went out at no cost).
    Estimates and sales orders are not checked.
 9. A processing order (Disassemble / Process Items) is not posted - Save and post,
    Complete and post - while an input has no unit cost or average unit cost, or a
    co-product or by-product would be received at no cost (scrap may be). The input
    shows "no unit cost" in red on the form and the output's Unit cost box is
    outlined in red. Save as pending is still allowed.
    Both checks can be switched off in System Administration > Company, Security &
    Closing Date > Inventory and POS: "Block sales of items with no unit cost" and
    "Block posting processing orders with no unit cost" (both on as delivered).

Notes

 - The settings and the messages that named the Chart of Accounts menu now say
   Financial Set-up & Reports.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
