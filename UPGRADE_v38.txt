G-SMaRT Accounting - version 38 (2027-01-14)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 37.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
1. Back up the database.
2. Run database/migrations/2027_01_14_cash_count_sheet.sql on it (phpMyAdmin:
   Import; Navicat: Execute SQL File). It works on MySQL 5.5 and can be run a
   second time. It adds:
    - the table pos_cash_counts: the bills and coins counted when a shift is
      closed (empty at first);
    - the report Point of Sale > Reports > Cash Count Sheet, for the roles that may
      open the X- and Z-Reading Summary, and for the cashiers (their own shifts).
   Nothing is deleted and no amount changes. Until it is run, the system stops
   after log-in with a page that names it.
3. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the page script and the style sheet changed).

What is new
-----------
1. Add new supplier on Receive Items and Enter Bills
 - The Vendor box at the top of Receive Items and Enter Bills (and of Receive Items
   and Enter Bill for Received Items) now ends with "Add new supplier", as on the
   other transactions. Saving the new supplier opens the page for that supplier.
   The same rule covers every box on a transaction page that only chooses the
   supplier or customer to work with.

2. POS Receipts, Returns & Voids
 - From and To are a date and a time ("From date and time", "To date and time"):
   a receipt counts by the moment it was rung up. They start on today, 12:00 AM to
   11:59 PM. An older link with dates only still works.
 - New Cashier filter. Choosing a cashier sets the dates and times by itself: from
   the opening of the cashier's first shift on the days chosen to the closing of
   the last one (now, for a shift still open); without a shift, the first and last
   receipt of the cashier on those days; else the cashier's latest shift. Only that
   cashier's receipts are listed.
 - Totals at the foot: the Total of the receipts (sales less returns, voided ones
   left out) with the number of receipts, and the Sales and Returns lines when
   there are returns. The totals count every receipt of the period, even when only
   the latest 500 are listed.
 - On a phone each receipt is a card with its total at the right.

3. Gross sales of a shift (Current shift, X- and Z-readings)
 - An item group sold at its group price: the price variance (group price less
   the prices of its items) is now part of the Gross sales, so Gross sales less
   Discounts agrees with Net sales + VAT when there is no VAT. Readings already
   printed keep their figures; the readings from now on have the corrected gross.

4. General Ledger: Cashier filter
 - Financial > Reports > General Ledger has a Cashier filter (the users who have
   entered POS transactions). Choosing one shows only the transactions that
   cashier entered, and the beginning balance is of that cashier's transactions
   alone. The heading of the report names the cashier.

5. Cash Count Sheet
 - Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings > Close register: clicking "Cash counted in
   drawer" (or the bills button next to it) opens the Cash Count Sheet, headed with
   the Date cover (shift opened to now) and the Cashier name. Type the quantity of
   each bill (1,000, 500, 200, 100, 50, 20) and coin (20, 10, 5, 1, 0.25, 0.10,
   0.05, 0.01); the amounts and the Total cash follow. "Use this count" puts the
   total in Cash counted in drawer. The quantities are kept with the shift.
 - Correcting the closing entries of a shift: the bills button next to Cash
   counted opens the sheet kept, to count again; it then replaces the sheet.
 - New report Point of Sale > Reports > Cash Count Sheet: one sheet per completed
   shift (Date cover, Cashier name, terminal, every bill and coin, Total cash,
   Expected cash in drawer, Over/(short)). Choose a Shift for one sheet, or list
   every completed shift of the dates with their total. A cashier sees their own
   shifts. The shift page and the list of recent shifts link to it.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v38.docx) are updated: Point of Sale (Cash Count
Sheet, POS Receipts, gross sales, the Cash Count Sheet report) and Financial
(General Ledger).
