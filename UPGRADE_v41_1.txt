G-SMaRT Accounting - version 41.1 (2027-02-05)
===============================================

A correction to version 41. Copy the accounting-system folder over your
installation, keeping the folder structure. No database update is needed.
Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the Community Chat script changed).

Community Chat: "Active now"
 - Before: a user was listed only while they had the Community Chat page itself
   open, so cashiers working at the POS Register (or anyone on another page)
   were not shown.
 - Now: everyone who has had any page of G-SMaRT Accounting open in the last
   five minutes, and who may use the Community Chat, is listed, whatever page
   they are on. A user who logs out leaves the list at once.
 - The list refreshes every 30 seconds while the chat is open.
