G-SMaRT Accounting - version 34 (2026-12-17)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 33.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
No database update this time: no new migration, no table or column change, and the
bundled database files are unchanged. (If 2026_12_10_class_reports.sql of version
33 was not run yet, run it.)

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What is new
-----------
Banking > Make Deposits > Payments waiting to be deposited (Undeposited Funds):
 - Journal entries that post to Undeposited Funds (Make Journal Entries, opening
   balance entries too) are listed with the customer payments and sales receipts,
   by date. They are usually adjustments of what the POS or the collections bring to
   the bank: a cash overage, a shortage, a correction.
 - Amount is the entry's net on Undeposited Funds. Method says "Debit, adds" or
   "Credit, deducts"; a credit shows in red in brackets.
 - Ticked, a debit adds to Items selected and the Deposit total, a credit deducts from
   them. The deposit gets a line on Undeposited Funds for the net (negative for a
   credit), linked to the entry, so the entry is deposited once and leaves the list.
 - An entry on a deposit cannot be voided or deleted until it is taken off the deposit
   or the deposit is voided ("It is included in deposit DEP-00012...").
 - Edit Deposit shows the entries it holds ticked; an entry changed since shows its new
   amount with "was" and the old one; saving takes the new amount.
 - A deposit cannot add up to less than zero: "The items ticked add up to less than
   zero (-120.00): a journal entry that credits Undeposited Funds deducts from the
   payments or POS cash it adjusts. Tick them on the same deposit."

Note: journal entries to Undeposited Funds made before this version that were already
taken care of by hand (for example with an Other receipts line on a deposit) are listed
too, since nothing links them to that deposit. Leave those unticked.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v34.docx) are updated.
