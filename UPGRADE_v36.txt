G-SMaRT Accounting - version 36 (2026-12-31)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 35.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
No database update this time: no new migration, no table or column change, and the
bundled database files are unchanged. (If 2026_12_24_fb_pos.sql of version 35 was
not run yet, run it.)

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What is new
-----------
1. A supplier on an expense line (Write Checks, Enter Bills, supplier credits,
   Enter Bill for Received Items)
 - The Customer:Job column of the Expenses tab is now "Customer / Supplier". Type
   part of a name: the list shows the customers and jobs and the suppliers, with
   "Customer" or "Supplier" after each name.
 - A supplier chosen on a line goes with that line into the general ledger, so the
   cost is on that supplier's subsidiary ledger and in Expenses by Vendor, even when
   the check is made out to someone else. The bank or A/P line keeps the payee.
 - Billable applies to customers only: it is cleared and switched off while a
   supplier is chosen on the line.
 - The saved check or bill shows the customer or supplier of each expense line.
 - The Items tab is unchanged (Customer:Job).

2. The 2% rule and the re-costing: Disassemble / Process Items only
 - The 2% check (outputs against inputs plus labor) is made on Disassemble /
   Process Items only. Bills, item receipts, checks, cash vouchers and the other
   transactions are never checked against it.
 - The automatic re-costing of version 35 is now started only by a processing order
   that is back-dated, edited, voided or deleted. A back-dated or edited bill, item
   receipt, check, sale, adjustment or transfer is saved at once, with no message
   such as "The costs of the 7,690 inventory transactions dated from 10/02/2026 were
   not worked out again".
 - System Administration > Company, Security & Closing Date > Inventory and POS >
   "Cost the later inventory transactions again ...": Disassemble / Process Items
   only (the standard), Every inventory transaction (as in version 35), or Never.
 - After entering a batch of back-dated bills or item receipts (for example at month
   end), run System Administration > Utilities: Verify & Rebuild Data > Rebuild data
   so the later costs follow them.
 - The messages now read "Verify & Rebuild Data" (they showed "Verify &amp; Rebuild
   Data").

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v36.docx) are updated: Vendors (Enter Bills),
Banking (Write Checks), Inventory (the 2% rule, costing again) and System
Administration (the settings).
