G-SMaRT Accounting - version 19 (2026-10-03)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 18.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Do NOT import database/accounting_system.sql or accounting_system_clean.sql over a
live database (they drop and recreate every table; they are for new installs only).
On a database already in use, run this one migration file (MySQL 5.5 safe, re-runnable):

    database/migrations/2026_10_03_categories_reports_shift_corrections.sql

With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin > Companies >
Database updates > Update all companies does that). Until it is run, the system stops
after log-in with a page that names it.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v19.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Inventory > Lists > Category List: add, rename and delete item categories, with
    the number of items, active items, quantity and value of each. A category in use
    is deleted only after its items are moved to another category or to none (which
    also merges two categories). The Item List can be filtered by category.
 2. Inventory Valuation Summary: an "Inventory site" filter. With a site chosen the
    quantities and values are that site's.
 3. Quantity on Hand by Site: an "As of" date filter (today by default).
 4. POS Daily Sales Summary: a Class filter (Unclassified = sales without a class).
 5. New reports: Income by Customer Summary and Income by Customer Detail (sales of
    the items sold to each customer less their cost: income or loss, and margin), and
    Expenses by Vendor Summary (by month, quarter or year if wanted) and Expenses by
    Vendor Detail (every expense charged to each supplier, with account and class).
 6. Point of Sale > Shifts, Cash Drops & Readings: an administrator or POS supervisor
    corrects the closing entries of a closed shift (opening float, cash drops, cash
    counted). Expected cash and over/short are worked out again, the Cash Over/Short
    journal entry is changed, made or voided to match, the Z-reading prints the new
    figures, and every correction is kept in the audit trail and listed on the shift.
 7. New report: No. of Days Inventory Has No Movement (days since each item was last
    sold to a customer, with its last movement of any kind; by site and category).
 8. Adjust Quantity/Value on Hand: an "Adjustment option". Per Batch Adjustment is the
    grid of every item (as before). Per Item Adjustment is the QuickBooks layout: item
    lines with qty on hand, new quantity or difference, U/M and class, Find & Select
    Items, Set Qty to Zero, Customer:Job, total value and number of item adjustments,
    Save & Close / Save & New / Clear. The choice is remembered.
 9. The opening cash float of the cashier is shown on the current shift, on the list
    of shifts, on each shift's page and on the X- and Z-readings.

Also: report group headings, subtotals and totals now run across the empty columns
after them (no more "Total ABC Trading Corp." squeezed into the Type column), and the
Supervisor approval box stacks its two boxes when it sits in a narrow card.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v19.docx) are updated for all of the above.
