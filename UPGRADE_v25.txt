G-SMaRT Accounting - version 25 (2026-10-22)
=============================================

This package holds only the files that were added or changed since version 24.
Copy the accounting-system folder over your installation, keeping the folder
structure. config/config.php is not included and is never changed.

Database
--------
Run ONE new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
Navicat: Execute SQL File), after backing it up:

    database/migrations/2026_10_22_inventory_movement_reports.sql

It adds four report pages: Inventory Movement Summary Report and Inventory Movement
Detailed Report (given to the roles that may open No. of Days Inventory Has No
Movement), Sales Report Through Inventory Movement (given to the roles that may open
Sales by Item Summary) and Inventory Movement Transactions, the drill-down page the
three reports open (given to both). No table or column is added, nothing is deleted
and no amount changes. Written for MySQL 5.5, safe to run twice. With several
companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies > Database
updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page naming it.

database/accounting_system.sql and accounting_system_clean.sql (new installs only)
include the change. Do NOT import those two files over a live database.

After copying the files, press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new
assets/js/wht.js and assets/css/app.css.

What is new (the requests of "ADDITIONAL_REVISED FEATURES ... v25.xlsx")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Inventory Movement Summary Report (Inventory > Reports). Filters: From and To
    dates, Site, Class (and Item, Category). Columns: Item code, Item description,
    Beg. balance (the ending inventory of the day before the From date), In, Out and
    Ending balance (the ending inventory of the To date = Beg. balance + In - Out),
    with a TOTAL line.
    In:  Write Checks (and Cash Vouchers) with inventory, positive or negative;
         Receive Items; Receive Items and Enter Bills; Transfer Inventory to the site;
         Adjust Quantity positive; Build Assemblies assembled items; Disassemble /
         Process Items outputs.
    Out: Create Invoices; Enter Sales Receipts; Create Batch Invoices; POS sales
         (less credit memos and POS returns that restock); Transfer Inventory from
         the site; Adjust Quantity negative; Build Assemblies component items;
         Disassemble / Process Items inputs consumed; Supplier Debit Memos.
    Only transactions dated within the range count, voided ones are left out. Every
    figure (the TOTAL line too) is a link to its source transactions.
 2. Inventory Movement Detailed Report (Inventory > Reports), same filters. Columns:
    Item code, Item description, Beg. balance; under an IN heading Purchases,
    Transfer inventory, Adjust quantity, Build assemblies, Disassemble / process
    items; under an OUT heading (negative figures) Sales, Transfer inventory, Adjust
    quantity, Build assemblies, Disassemble / process items, Supplier debit memo; and
    Ending balance (the sum of the line). TOTAL line; every figure drills down.
 3. Sales Report Through Inventory Movement (Customers > Reports, Sales Reports).
    Filters: From and To dates, Site, Class, Cashier (and Item, Category). Columns:
    Item description, Beg. inventory, Delivery (in), Withdrawals (out), Ending
    inventory, Sales (qty) = Beg. + Delivery - Withdrawals - Ending, Ave. price (the
    average price per unit of the invoices, batch invoices, sales receipts and POS
    sales) and Total sales = Sales (qty) x Ave. price, which equals the amount of
    those documents. Delivery and Withdrawals leave out invoices, batch invoices,
    sales receipts and POS sales. TOTAL line (its Ave. price is total sales over the
    quantity sold). With a Cashier chosen, the sales columns are that cashier's
    documents and a "Sold by other cashiers" column shows the rest of the quantity
    sold. Every figure drills down.
 4. Inventory Movement Transactions: the page the figures open, listing the
    transactions (type, date, number, name, site, movement, quantity) item by item,
    with a total equal to the figure clicked. Click a number to open the transaction.
 5. Pay Bills (sheet 4): the Withholding box works out the tax base from the bill's
    outstanding balance (net of VAT, less the early-payment discount) as soon as it is
    opened, even when the bill is not ticked. Ticking a tax computes its amount at
    once, ticks the bill and lowers the amount to pay; the amount can still be typed
    over for an adjustment. Edit Bill Payment and Receive Payments work the same way.

The user's manual (Help button, Home > User's Manual) and the Word edition
(GSMaRT_Accounting_Users_Manual_v25.docx) are updated for all of the above.
